Basics

What happens after death?

Christianity’s hope is not mainly floating in heaven forever. It is resurrection.

Christians believe death is real, judgment is real, and death will not get the last word.

Resurrection is the center.

The New Testament points toward God raising the dead and renewing creation. Heaven matters, but the final Christian hope is embodied life with God in a restored creation.

What about judgment and hell?

Christians agree that God judges evil and that rejecting God has real consequences. They disagree about some details of hell: its nature, imagery, and duration.

I would rather name that disagreement than pretend every Christian has always explained every passage the same way.

Why does any of this matter now?

Because Christianity treats death as an enemy, human life as valuable, justice as something God will not ignore, and resurrection as a promise rather than a metaphor.
