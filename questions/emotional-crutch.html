Questions · Doubt & motives

Is Christianity just emotional comfort for people afraid of death?

Short answer: Christianity can absolutely be comforting. That does not tell us whether it is true. A belief can be comforting and true, or comforting and false.

The strongest objection: humans have obvious reasons to want death not to be the end, to want justice, and to want someone in control. Those desires can bias belief.

What I’m not sure about: Christians are not immune to wishful thinking. That is one reason historical claims—especially the resurrection—matter. Christianity does not get to be true merely because we need it to be.

Did this miss the point? If this answer is confusing, incomplete, or wrong, tell me.
