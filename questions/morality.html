I want to follow Jesus

Questions · God & belief

Is something good because God commands it—or does God command it because it’s good?

Short answer: the usual Christian answer is neither. God does not invent goodness arbitrarily, and goodness is not a standard above God. Christians say goodness is rooted in God’s own character.

The Bible describes God as righteous, truthful, and unchanging, and Christians point to Jesus as the clearest picture of what God is like (Deuteronomy 32:4 · Titus 1:2 · John 14:9).

The strongest objection: saying “God’s character is good” can sound circular. How do we know the character attributed to God is actually good rather than simply defining goodness as whatever God is?

What I’m not sure about: a definition alone does not solve that objection. Christianity eventually has to point to the God revealed in Jesus and ask whether that character is actually worthy of being called good.
