Questions · Jesus & resurrection

Did Jesus predict He would return within His disciples’ lifetimes?

Short answer: it depends on what “coming” means, and the Gospels don’t make that easy. Some readers think Jesus was wrong. Others point to the Transfiguration, to the resurrection and Pentecost, or to Jesus’ vindication in the terms of Daniel 7 (Matthew 16:28 · Matthew 17:1–2 · Mark 9:1).

The strongest objection: “some standing here will not taste death” sounds like a prediction that the kingdom would arrive within the lifetime of Jesus’ hearers. Taken at face value, that is a prediction that did not come true.

What I’m not sure about: which reading is right. The Transfiguration fits the placement but strains at “taste death.” The Daniel 7 reading fits the word “coming” but has trouble with the verse right before it, which sounds like final judgment.
