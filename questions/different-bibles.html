Why do Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant Bibles contain different books?

Short answer: the disagreement is mainly about Old Testament books often called the Deuterocanon or Apocrypha. Different Christian traditions inherited and evaluated those books differently.

The strongest objection: if God gave a book to guide the church, disagreement about which books belong in it looks awkward.

What I’m not sure about: the differences are real, but the central story of Jesus does not depend on them. Still, pretending every Christian has always had exactly the same table of contents would be historically false.
