Who decided which books belong in the Bible?

Short answer: no single person sat down and invented the Bible. The Old Testament canon developed within Judaism. Early Christians received those Scriptures and gradually recognized a collection of apostolic writings as authoritative.

The strongest objection: there were disputes. Some books were accepted earlier than others, and Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant traditions still differ over parts of the Old Testament.

What I’m not sure about: “the church chose the Bible” and “the Bible dropped fully formed from heaven” are both bad summaries. Canon was a historical process, and Christians interpret that process differently.
