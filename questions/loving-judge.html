Questions · God & belief

If God is loving, why does He judge people?

Short answer: love and judgment are not necessarily opposites. If God loves people, Christians argue He must take seriously what destroys people: cruelty, exploitation, lies, abuse, violence, and evil.

The strongest objection: divine judgment can become terrifying once punishment is eternal or once people are judged for unbelief, not just obvious cruelty.

What I’m not sure about: I think Christianity needs both mercy and judgment to make moral sense. The exact nature and duration of final judgment is one place Christians disagree more than they sometimes admit.
