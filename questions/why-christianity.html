Why Christianity instead of Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, or Buddhism?

Short answer: Christianity deserves to be examined because it makes a concrete claim about Jesus: that He was crucified, that His followers came to believe He was alive again, and that who He is changes what is true about God and us. If that claim fails, Christianity loses its center.

That gives you something more specific to investigate than “which religion feels best?” Christianity asks you to look at Jesus—His identity, death, and resurrection—and decide whether the claims about Him hold up.

The strongest objection: most people inherit religion from family and geography, Christians included. That should make everyone suspicious of believing whatever happened to be nearby.

What I’m not sure about: comparing religions fairly takes more than listing differences. Christianity should be tested on its strongest case, and Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism, and other traditions deserve the same courtesy.
