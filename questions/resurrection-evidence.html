Questions · Jesus & resurrection

What evidence do we actually have that Jesus rose from the dead?

Short answer: the case is historical rather than scientific. The earliest Christian sources say Jesus died, was buried, was raised, and appeared to named people and groups; Paul preserves one such summary in 1 Corinthians 15:3–8.

Christians also point to the empty-tomb tradition, the claimed appearances, James’s change from unbelief to being named as a witness, and Paul’s change from persecutor to apostle (John 7:5 · 1 Corinthians 15:7 · Galatians 1:13–16).

The strongest objection: early and sincere testimony is still testimony. People can be mistaken, and history cannot rerun the event in a lab.

What I’m not sure about: the evidence can get you to very early resurrection belief and reported appearances. Whether resurrection is the best explanation also depends on whether you think a miracle is possible at all.
