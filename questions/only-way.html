Questions · Other religions & salvation

Is Jesus really the only way to God?

Short answer: the New Testament does claim that Jesus is the only way. Jesus says no one comes to the Father except through Him (John 14:6). Peter says salvation is found in no one else (Acts 4:12). Paul says there is one mediator between God and people, the man Christ Jesus (1 Timothy 2:5). The harder question is what that means for people who have never heard of Him, and Christians don’t all answer it the same way.

Three main Christian answers:

  • Exclusivism: salvation comes only through Jesus, and a person has to hear about Him and trust Him in this life.
  • Inclusivism: salvation comes only through what Jesus did, but God can save people who never learned His name and who respond to what they do know of Him. The Catholic Church says so in Lumen Gentium 16 (1964). C. S. Lewis pictures it in The Last Battle (1956). Many Protestants and Orthodox also hold versions of it.
  • Pluralism: every major religion is a valid route to God (John Hick, An Interpretation of Religion, 1989). This is a minority view among Christians, because it requires setting aside what Jesus says about Himself.

The Bible also says God wants everyone to be saved (1 Timothy 2:4), that people are judged by what they were given (Romans 2:12–16), and, in Abraham’s words, that the Judge of all the earth will do what is right (Genesis 18:25).

The strongest objection: it sounds arrogant. Billions of sincere people follow other religions, and a God who is good wouldn’t make salvation depend on getting the right belief. Part of this is a fair point about how Christians have often behaved. But a narrow claim isn’t automatically an arrogant one. Jesus’ claim is about Himself, not about the moral superiority of His followers. If He is who He said He is, the claim isn’t arrogance but information. And the other religions also disagree with each other on central points. See Aren’t all religions basically different paths to the same God?

What I’m not sure about: I don’t know exactly how God will judge people who never heard, or whether some will be saved without ever knowing Jesus’ name, and I distrust anyone who claims to know. I do hold that God is just and merciful, and that this is the character Jesus shows. For related questions, see What happens to people who never hear about Jesus?, Why would salvation depend on believing the right thing? and Why Christianity instead of Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, or Buddhism?
