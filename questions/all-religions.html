Questions · Other religions & salvation

Aren’t all religions basically different paths to the same God?

Short answer: that sounds generous, but it usually flattens the religions themselves. Buddhism does not simply say what Christianity says in different vocabulary. Islam and Christianity disagree directly about Jesus’ identity and crucifixion.

The strongest objection: maybe no tradition has the whole picture, and each one reflects part of a larger reality.

What I’m not sure about: that possibility cannot just be dismissed. But “all paths are the same” is itself a religious claim—and one the religions generally do not make about themselves.
