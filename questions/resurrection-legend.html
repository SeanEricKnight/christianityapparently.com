Questions · Jesus & resurrection

Could the resurrection stories have grown into legend?

Short answer: they could have developed in retelling, but the basic resurrection claim appears very early. Paul’s letters, written before the Gospels, already treat Jesus’ death and resurrection as central and name witnesses (1 Corinthians 15:3–8).

The strongest objection: early belief is not the same thing as a true belief. People can sincerely believe extraordinary things.

What I’m not sure about: “legend” does not explain everything by itself. The harder question is what best explains the combination of early proclamation, claimed appearances, and the transformed movement.
