Questions · Other religions & salvation

If Christianity is true, why does where you’re born matter so much?

It does matter. Culture shapes what feels believable to everyone, Christians included. But how you came to believe something is a separate question from whether it’s true.

The Bible says God made the nations to seek Him (Acts 17:26–27), people are judged by what they were given (Luke 12:48; Romans 2:12–16), Abraham was blessed so “all peoples on earth” would be blessed (Genesis 12:3), and God “wants all people to be saved” (1 Timothy 2:4).

Abraham asks the question Christians still have to ask: will the Judge of all the earth do what is right? (Genesis 18:25).

The Bible doesn’t spell out how this works for every person, so be careful around anyone who says they know.
