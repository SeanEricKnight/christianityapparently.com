If Christianity is true, why does where you’re born matter so much?

Short answer: where you are born clearly affects what you are likely to believe. Christianity does not get to pretend otherwise. But how a belief reached you and whether it is true are still different questions.

The Bible says God made the nations to seek Him, that people are judged in light of what they were given, and that His concern extends to all peoples (Acts 17:26–27 · Luke 12:48 · 1 Timothy 2:4).

The strongest objection: religious belief tracks family, culture, and geography strongly enough that confidence should make all of us uncomfortable.

What I’m not sure about: the Bible does not give a complete map for how God judges every person in every circumstance. I am more comfortable with Abraham’s question—will the Judge of all the earth do what is right?—than with pretending I know every edge case (Genesis 18:25).
