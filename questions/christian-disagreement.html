If the Holy Spirit guides Christians, why do sincere Christians disagree so much?

Jesus promised guidance into truth (John 14:26; John 16:13), not that every Christian would agree on every interpretation.

Paul says “we know in part” (1 Corinthians 13:9–12), and 2 Peter 3:15–16 says some of Paul’s writing is hard to understand. The early church disagreed too: Paul publicly confronted Peter (Galatians 2:11–14), Paul and Barnabas split over a coworker (Acts 15:36–41), and the council in Acts 15 had to argue its way through a major dispute.

Romans 14 also leaves room for believers to differ on some matters. Disagreement doesn’t prove there’s no guidance, but it should make all of us less sure of ourselves. Test claims instead of assuming (1 John 4:1).
