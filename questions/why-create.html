If God knew humanity would fall, why create us anyway?

The Bible presents redemption as something God wasn’t improvising after things went wrong. Believers are described as chosen in Christ “before the creation of the world” (Ephesians 1:4), and 1 Peter 1:18–20 describes Christ as chosen before creation.

Creation is called “very good” (Genesis 1:31), creation is subjected to frustration “in hope” (Romans 8:20–21), and Paul says grace outran sin: “where sin increased, grace increased all the more” (Romans 5:20).

The Christian claim is that a world with freedom, love, rebellion, rescue, and in the end a restored creation was worth making—and that in Jesus, God didn’t stay safely outside the cost (Philippians 2:5–8). That’s a claim of faith, not a proof.
