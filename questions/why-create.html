I want to follow Jesus

Questions · God & belief

If God knew humanity would fall, why create us anyway?

Short answer: Christianity says creation was worth making even though God knew rebellion and suffering would follow, and that rescue was not an improvised Plan B.

The Bible calls creation good, speaks of God’s purpose in Christ as older than the world, and points toward creation being restored rather than abandoned (Genesis 1:31 · Ephesians 1:4 · Romans 8:20–21).

The strongest objection: saying the final good is worth the suffering can sound easy when we are talking about suffering someone else had to endure.

What I’m not sure about: I cannot prove that a world containing freedom, love, rebellion, rescue, and eventual restoration was worth the cost. Christianity’s answer is partly that God did not remain outside that cost; in Jesus, He entered it (Philippians 2:5–8).
