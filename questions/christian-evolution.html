Can you believe in evolution and still be a Christian?

Short answer: yes. Many Christians accept evolutionary science while believing God created and sustains the world.

The strongest objection: evolution raises hard questions about Adam and Eve, death before the fall, human uniqueness, and how to read Genesis.

What I’m not sure about: there is no single Christian model everyone accepts. The disagreement is not really “science versus Christianity”; it is often about how particular biblical texts should be interpreted.
