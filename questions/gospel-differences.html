Questions · Jesus & resurrection

Why do the resurrection accounts differ?

Short answer: the resurrection accounts agree on the central claim but differ in details and emphasis.

They agree that Jesus was crucified and buried, women went to the tomb, the tomb was found empty, and His followers said He appeared alive. They vary over who is named at the tomb, how many angelic figures are described, and where particular appearances are emphasized (Matthew 28 · Mark 16 · Luke 24 · John 20–21).

The strongest objection: some differences are harder to harmonize than others, and that matters when the claim itself is extraordinary.

What I’m not sure about: I do not think every difference needs to be forced into one seamless timeline. The real question is whether those differences overturn the shared core or show that the accounts are independent enough not to read like copies.
