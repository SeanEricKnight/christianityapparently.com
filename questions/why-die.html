Questions · Jesus & resurrection

Why did Jesus have to die? Couldn’t God just forgive us?

Short answer: Christianity says God forgives, but forgiveness does not mean pretending evil cost nothing. At the cross, Christians believe God absorbs the cost Himself in Jesus.

The New Testament uses several pictures for what Jesus’ death accomplishes: ransom, sacrifice, reconciliation, and victory over evil and death (Mark 10:45 · 2 Corinthians 5:18–21 · Colossians 2:13–15).

The strongest objection: why should forgiveness require blood or death at all? Could an all-powerful God not simply forgive?

What I’m not sure about: Christians disagree about exactly how the different pictures of the cross fit together. The center is simpler than the theories: “while we were still sinners, Christ died for us” (Romans 5:8).
