Questions · Jesus & resurrection

What if someone stole Jesus’ body?

Short answer: a stolen body could explain an empty tomb, but not by itself the reported appearances or the disciples’ conviction that Jesus had been raised.

The strongest objection: the empty-tomb tradition itself has to be evaluated. If the burial or tomb details are unreliable, there may be no body-theft problem to solve.

What I’m not sure about: no single alternative theory answers every piece of the evidence neatly. That does not automatically prove resurrection, but it is why Christians think the case is cumulative rather than one clever argument.
