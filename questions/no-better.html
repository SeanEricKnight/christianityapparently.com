Questions · Christians & church

If Christianity changes people, why are Christians often no better than everyone else?

Short answer: Christianity does not claim Christians begin as better people. It claims God is changing them.

The strongest objection: if the change is real, eventually it should be visible. Claims about transformation are hollow if Christians are just as dishonest, cruel, greedy, or abusive as everyone else.

What I’m not sure about: measuring moral transformation across millions of people is difficult. At the personal level, though, the question is fair: if following Jesus never changes how I treat people, something is wrong.

Did this miss the point? If this answer is confusing, incomplete, or wrong, tell me.
