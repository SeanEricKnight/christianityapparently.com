Questions · Suffering & judgment

What happens to babies or children who die?

Short answer: the Bible does not give a single explicit verse that answers every question here. Many Christians trust that children who die are received by God’s mercy.

The strongest objection: that conclusion often rests on broader beliefs about God’s character rather than a direct statement.

What I’m not sure about: I would not pretend certainty where Scripture is not explicit. I am comfortable saying that God is more just and merciful than we are, not less.
