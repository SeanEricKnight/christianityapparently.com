Questions · God & belief

Does God actually exist, or are humans just wired to invent Him?

Short answer: Christianity does not prove God the way you prove a geometry theorem. It argues that God makes better sense of reality than the alternatives: why anything exists at all, why the universe is intelligible, why consciousness and moral obligation are real, and why human beings keep reaching beyond survival for meaning.

The strongest objection: human beings are very good at pattern-making, agency-detection, and inventing explanations. Religion could be one more example of that.

What I’m not sure about: no single argument gets you all the way to the God Christians worship. For Christianity, the case eventually has to move from abstract “God” to Jesus.
