Questions · God & belief

Why does God want or deserve worship?

Short answer: Christian worship is not supposed to be feeding a divine ego. It is recognizing what is actually true: that God is God and we are not. Worship includes gratitude, trust, obedience, awe, and love—not merely singing compliments.

The strongest objection: a being who demands worship can sound insecure or authoritarian.

What I’m not sure about: Christians sometimes talk about worship in ways that make God sound needy. The Bible also says God is not served as though He needed anything (Acts 17:24–25).
