Questions · Other religions & salvation

Why would salvation depend on believing the right thing?

Short answer: Many Christians understand faith as trusting Jesus, not getting every religious answer right. Trust matters because salvation is relationship and allegiance: trusting Jesus, not merely agreeing that certain propositions are probably true.

The strongest objection: belief is not fully voluntary. You cannot simply decide to find a claim convincing.

What I’m not sure about: I think God can distinguish stubborn refusal from honest inability to believe. Christians should be careful about pretending we know exactly where that line falls in another person.
