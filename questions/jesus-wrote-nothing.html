Questions · Jesus & resurrection

Why didn’t Jesus write anything Himself?

The Bible doesn’t say why. The only time the Gospels describe Him writing is in the dirt (John 8:6). He taught out loud and trained witnesses to carry His teaching forward (Matthew 28:19–20; Acts 1:8).

Luke says he drew on what eyewitnesses and earlier witnesses “handed down” (Luke 1:1–4), and John says his Gospel was written so people would believe (John 20:30–31; John 21:24–25).

So Christianity rests on testimony about Jesus, and that means you can’t avoid the historical question of whether that testimony is reliable.
