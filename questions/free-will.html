I want to follow Jesus

Questions · God & belief

If God already knows every choice I’ll make, am I really free?

Christians disagree here. Knowing what someone will do isn’t obviously the same as making them do it, but that doesn’t settle the whole philosophical problem.

The Bible often holds divine purpose and human responsibility together (Acts 2:23; Genesis 50:20). It also commands real choice (Deuteronomy 30:19; Joshua 24:15) and records Jesus grieving, “you were not willing” (Matthew 23:37).

Christians divide over models—Calvinist, Arminian, Molinist, open-theist, and others. None of them is universally accepted. It’s a real puzzle, and it hasn’t been solved.
