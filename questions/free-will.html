Questions · God & belief

If God already knows every choice I’ll make, am I really free?

Short answer: Christians disagree. God knowing a choice is not obviously the same thing as God causing the choice, but that does not settle the whole problem.

The Bible often puts divine purpose and human responsibility beside each other without explaining the mechanics, while still speaking as if human choices matter (Acts 2:23 · Genesis 50:20 · Joshua 24:15).

The strongest objection: if the future is infallibly known, it is hard to see in what sense you could genuinely do otherwise.

What I’m not sure about: Calvinist, Arminian, Molinist, open-theist, and other models try to solve this differently. No one model is accepted by all Christians, and the philosophical problem is real.
