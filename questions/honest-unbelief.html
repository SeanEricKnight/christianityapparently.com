Questions · Doubt & motives

What happens if someone honestly searches for God and still can’t believe?

Short answer: the New Testament does not treat honest doubt as the same thing as stubborn refusal. Jesus helps the man who says, “I believe; help my unbelief,” and gives Thomas the evidence he asked for (Mark 9:24 · John 20:24–29).

The strongest objection: belief is not fully voluntary. You cannot simply order yourself to find a claim convincing, which makes any account of judgment based on belief difficult.

What I’m not sure about: I do not know exactly where God draws the line between honest inability and refusal. I do think Christians should be much slower than they often are to claim they can see that line inside someone else.
