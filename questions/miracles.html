Questions · Science & reason

If miracles violate nature, why believe they happened?

Short answer: Christians do not usually mean that miracles are nature malfunctioning. They mean the God who ordinarily sustains nature can act within it in unusual ways.

The strongest objection: extraordinary claims require strong evidence, and miracle stories appear in many religions.

What I’m not sure about: calling something a miracle should come after investigation, not before it. Christianity stakes a lot on one miracle in particular—the resurrection—and that claim deserves more scrutiny than “weird thing happened, therefore God.”
