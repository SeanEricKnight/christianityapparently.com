Questions · God & belief

If God wants us to know Him, why is He so hard to find?

The Bible admits this happens. Isaiah 45:15 describes God as hidden. The Psalms complain about it constantly (Psalm 10:1; Psalm 13:1; Psalm 22:1; Psalm 88).

It also claims God has left evidence in creation (Psalm 19:1–4; Romans 1:19–20), and Acts 17:26–27 says people are meant to seek God and find Him.

The main Christian claim is that Jesus is God’s clearest way of showing Himself (John 14:9; Hebrews 1:1–2). The Bible also says more proof wouldn’t automatically change anyone’s mind (Luke 16:31). The promise is “seek and you will find” (Matthew 7:7; Jeremiah 29:13), though it doesn’t say when. I don’t know why it takes some people so long, and I’d be suspicious of anyone who says they do.
