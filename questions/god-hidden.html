I want to follow Jesus

Questions · God & belief

If God wants us to know Him, why is He so hard to find?

Short answer: the Bible does not pretend God always feels obvious. It contains people asking why He seems hidden, while also claiming that creation and, above all, Jesus give real reasons to seek Him (Psalm 13:1 · Acts 17:26–27 · John 14:9).

The strongest objection: if God wants to be known, the amount of ambiguity is hard to explain. Some people seek sincerely and still do not feel that they have found Him.

What I’m not sure about: I do not know why God seems unmistakable to some people and distant to others. The Bible gives permission to ask that question instead of pretending the distance is not there.
