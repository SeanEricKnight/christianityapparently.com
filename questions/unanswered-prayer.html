Why do some prayers seem to go unanswered?

The Bible gives several answers, and none of them is a formula.

  • Sometimes the answer is no: Paul asked three times for his thorn to be removed and got “my grace is sufficient” (2 Corinthians 12:7–10).
  • Sometimes the answer is to keep asking (Luke 18:1–8).
  • Sometimes we’re asking for the wrong reasons (James 4:2–3).
  • Jesus Himself prayed “take this cup from me” and didn’t get the way out He asked for (Matthew 26:39).
  • Sometimes there’s just silence, and the Psalms say so out loud (Psalm 13; Psalm 88).

Jesus does say God gives good gifts (Matthew 7:7–11), but that isn’t a guarantee you’ll get whatever you ask for. Romans 8:26 says the Spirit prays for us when we don’t have words.
