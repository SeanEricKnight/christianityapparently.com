Why do some prayers seem to go unanswered?

Short answer: the Bible does not give one formula for unanswered prayer. Sometimes the answer is no, sometimes wait, sometimes keep asking, and sometimes there is only silence.

Paul asked repeatedly for suffering to be removed and did not get the answer he wanted (2 Corinthians 12:7–10). Jesus prayed for another way in Gethsemane and still went to the cross (Matthew 26:39). The Psalms also say the silence out loud (Psalm 13 · Psalm 88).

The strongest objection: “God said no” can become a convenient explanation that cannot be tested and can sound cruel when someone is desperate.

What I’m not sure about: I do not know why some prayers seem answered and others do not. Christianity gives room to keep praying without pretending the silence is easy.
