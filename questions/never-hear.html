What happens to people who never hear about Jesus?

Short answer: Christians agree that God is just and that salvation is through Christ. They disagree about exactly how those truths apply to someone who never had a meaningful chance to hear the Gospel.

The strongest objection: if explicit belief in Jesus is required, geography and history seem to determine eternal destiny far too much.

What I’m not sure about: Scripture does not give us a chart for every edge case. I am more confident in God’s justice than I am in anybody’s tidy theory of how every unevangelized person is judged.
