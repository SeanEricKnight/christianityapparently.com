Questions · Suffering & judgment

Why does a loving, all-powerful God let children suffer?

Short answer: I do not think Christianity gives a neat explanation for why a particular child suffers. It says the world is not the way it is supposed to be, that God takes evil seriously, that He entered suffering in Jesus, and that death will not have the last word.

The strongest objection: that still leaves the immediate question: why did an all-powerful God allow this suffering in the first place?

What I’m not sure about: I do not know why God permits particular tragedies, and I do not trust explanations that make someone else’s pain sound tidy.
