Questions · Science & reason

Has science made belief in God obsolete?

Short answer: science has replaced many bad explanations of how things work. It has not demonstrated that God does not exist.

The strongest objection: as natural explanations expand, “God did it” can look like an ever-shrinking placeholder for ignorance.

What I’m not sure about: I think “God of the gaps” reasoning is weak. Christian belief is supposed to be about why there is a world with regular laws at all, and about what happened in Jesus—not about using God to patch holes in chemistry or physics.
