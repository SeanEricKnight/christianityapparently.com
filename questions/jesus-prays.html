Questions · Jesus & resurrection

If Jesus is God, why does He pray to God?

Short answer: Christianity does not say Jesus is the Father. It says the Son and the Father are distinct while sharing the one divine identity Christians call God.

It also says Jesus became genuinely human, so His prayer is not theater. The Gospels repeatedly show Him praying to the Father, including in Gethsemane and John 17 (Matthew 28:19 · Mark 14:36 · John 17).

The strongest objection: if Jesus is fully God, prayer can still sound like evidence that He is a lesser being talking to the real God.

What I’m not sure about: the Trinity explains why Jesus can pray without Christianity collapsing into two gods, but it does not make the relationship simple. The claim is that the Son lived a real human life of trust in the Father.
