Questions · Jesus & resurrection

Did Jesus really claim to be God?

Short answer: Jesus rarely gives the modern sentence “I am God,” but the Gospels portray Him doing and claiming things that, in Jewish context, belong to God: forgiving sins, judging the world, receiving worship, claiming unique unity with the Father, and using language tied to God’s identity.

The strongest objection: John’s Gospel is more explicit than the Synoptics, which raises the question of whether the highest claims developed later.

What I’m not sure about: the wording develops across the Gospels. The question is whether the development invents something new or makes explicit what was already there.
