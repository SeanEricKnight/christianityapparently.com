Questions · Jesus & resurrection

If Jesus is God, how can He not know something the Father knows?

Short answer: the New Testament presents Jesus as fully divine and genuinely human, and that includes real human limitation. Mark 13:32 says the Son does not know the day or hour (Mark 13:32).

Other passages say Jesus grew in wisdom, “emptied himself,” and was tested as we are (Luke 2:52 · Philippians 2:6–8 · Hebrews 4:15).

The strongest objection: saying “fully God” while also saying Jesus lacked knowledge sounds contradictory unless the incarnation can make sense of both claims.

What I’m not sure about: traditional theology gives the shape of the answer—one person who is fully divine and fully human—but it does not remove every philosophical puzzle about how Jesus’ knowledge worked.
