Questions · Suffering & judgment

Why would God create someone He knows will end up in hell?

Short answer: Christians disagree. Knowing that someone will reject God is not obviously the same as causing that rejection, but that does not remove the weight of creating a person while knowing the outcome.

The Bible also insists that God does not delight in people being lost and wants people to turn and live (Ezekiel 33:11 · 2 Peter 3:9 · 1 Timothy 2:4).

The strongest objection: foreknowledge still leaves the question: why create someone at all if God knows their life ends in judgment?

What I’m not sure about: Calvinists, Arminians, and others answer this differently. Romans 9–11 wrestles with judgment and mercy and ends in awe rather than a tidy mechanism. I do not think any system makes the question emotionally weightless.
