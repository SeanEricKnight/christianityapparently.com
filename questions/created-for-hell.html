Questions · Suffering & judgment

Why would God create someone He knows will end up in hell?

Christians disagree here. Knowing something will happen isn’t the same as causing it, but that doesn’t fully solve the problem.

Scripture stresses that God takes no pleasure in anyone being lost (Ezekiel 33:11; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 2:4). Paul wrestles openly with judgment and mercy in Romans 9–11 and ends in awe, not with a tidy explanation (Romans 11:32–36).

Jesus says of Judas, “It would be better for him if he had not been born” (Matthew 26:24), Nobody in the story treats that as a small thing. Calvinists, Arminians, and others answer the larger question differently. They all insist that God is good and judges rightly (Genesis 18:25), but none removes the weight of the question.
