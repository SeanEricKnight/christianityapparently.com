Questions · Jesus & resurrection

How is it justice to punish an innocent Jesus for guilty people?

It would be unjust if Jesus were an unrelated third party forced to take the hit. The New Testament says something different: “God was in Christ reconciling the world to himself” (2 Corinthians 5:19).

Jesus also describes His death as voluntary: “No one takes it from me, but I lay it down of my own accord” (John 10:17–18; see also Philippians 2:5–8).

So it’s closer to the judge bearing the cost Himself than to the judge punishing a random innocent person. Romans 3:25–26 says the cross is meant to show God as both just and the one who justifies. Christian traditions frame that in different ways—substitution, victory, restoration, and others—and the objection deserves to be taken seriously.
