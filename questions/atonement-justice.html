Questions · Jesus & resurrection

How is it justice to punish an innocent Jesus for guilty people?

Short answer: it would be unjust if God punished an unrelated innocent person instead of the guilty. Christianity claims something different: God Himself is acting in Christ to reconcile people to Himself (2 Corinthians 5:19).

Jesus also describes His death as voluntary, not something forced on Him against His will (John 10:17–18).

The strongest objection: even if Jesus is willing and divine, substitution can still sound morally strange: why should one person’s suffering deal with another person’s guilt?

What I’m not sure about: Christian traditions explain the cross with several models—substitution, victory, reconciliation, restoration, and others. I think the objection deserves more than one slogan.
