Questions · Jesus & resurrection

Why trust Gospel writers if the books were written decades later and do not name their authors in the text?

Short answer: the Gospels are ancient biographies written within living memory of Jesus, using earlier sources, eyewitness tradition, and named people. Their traditional titles are early, but the books themselves do not open with modern bylines.

The strongest objection: decades allow memory to shift, communities to shape stories, and theology to influence retelling.

What I’m not sure about: “anonymous” can be overstated, and so can “eyewitness.” The real work is comparing the texts, their sources, their differences, and what kind of historical claims they are actually making.
