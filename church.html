Find a church

Find people to follow Jesus with.

Not a perfect church. Those remain unavailable.

Christianity was never meant to be a solo project. A healthy church gives you people to worship with, learn with, serve with, argue with occasionally, and keep showing up with.

What should I look for?

Look for a church that takes Jesus and Scripture seriously, teaches the Gospel clearly, treats people with mercy, has accountable leadership, and does not panic when someone asks a difficult question.

Visit more than once if you can. Talk to actual people. Ask what they believe about Jesus, Scripture, baptism, communion, money, leadership, and what happens when someone fails badly.
