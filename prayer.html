Prayer

How do
I pray?

Start by telling the truth.

Prayer is not a performance review. Talk to God honestly.

Tell Him what is true.

Thank Him. Ask for help. Admit what you did. Tell Him you are angry, afraid, or not even sure He is there.

A perfectly usable prayer

God, I don’t know if You’re there. But if You are, I’m listening.

If you have no words

Borrow some. Read a Psalm slowly: Psalm 13 when God seems absent, Psalm 23 when you are afraid, Psalm 51 when you have failed, Psalm 88 when everything is dark.

Keep praying. Not to force God’s hand, but to stay in the conversation.

What if I’m not sure God is there?↗Look at Jesus.↗
