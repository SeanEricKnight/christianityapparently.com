Prayer

How do
I pray?

Start by telling the truth.

Prayer is not a performance review. You are not trying to impress God with vocabulary He has already heard.

Talk to Him.

Tell God what is true. Thank Him. Ask for help. Admit what you did. Tell Him you are angry. Tell Him you are afraid. Tell Him you are not even sure He is there.

You do not have to sound religious.

A perfectly usable prayer

God, I don’t know if You’re there. But if You are, I’m listening.

Jesus gave us a pattern.

The Lord’s Prayer begins with God—His name, His kingdom, His will—then moves into ordinary human need: food, forgiveness, temptation, evil.

Prayer can be that large and that ordinary at the same time.

What if nothing happens?

Sometimes prayer brings immediate clarity or comfort. Sometimes it doesn’t. Christianity does not promise that every prayer will be answered in the way we asked or on the schedule we wanted.

Keep praying anyway—not because repetition forces God’s hand, but because relationship includes staying in the conversation when you do not understand the silence.

If you have no words

Borrow somebody else’s. Read a Psalm slowly. Psalm 13 when God seems absent. Psalm 23 when you are afraid. Psalm 51 when you have failed. Psalm 88 when everything is dark and nobody puts a bow on it at the end.
