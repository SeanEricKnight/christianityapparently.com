Prayer

How do
I pray?

Just tell Him the truth.

Prayer isn’t a performance review. Talk to God honestly.

Tell Him what’s going on.

Thank Him for something. Ask for help. Admit what you did. Tell Him you’re angry, or scared, or not sure He’s even there.

A perfectly usable prayer

God, I don’t know if You’re there. But if You are, I’m listening.

If you have no words.

Borrow some. Read a Psalm slowly. Psalm 13 is for when God seems absent, Psalm 23 for when you’re afraid, Psalm 51 for when you’ve done something you’re ashamed of, Psalm 88 for when everything is dark.

Keep praying. It isn’t a way to force God’s hand. It’s more like staying in a conversation.
