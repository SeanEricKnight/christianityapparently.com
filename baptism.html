I want to follow Jesus

Christian practice

What is baptism?

A public act involving water, allegiance to Jesus, and a surprising amount of denominational disagreement about the plumbing.

Jesus told His followers to baptize disciples. Christians have treated baptism as basic from the beginning.

What does it mean?

Baptism marks identification with Jesus—His death and resurrection—and entrance into the visible life of the church.

It is a way of saying, publicly, “I belong to Jesus.”

Do Christians agree on how it works?

No. Some baptize only professing believers. Others baptize infants of Christian families. Some immerse; others pour or sprinkle. Traditions also differ on what baptism means for forgiveness and a person’s relationship with God.

Those are real disagreements, but almost every historic Christian tradition agrees that baptism matters.
