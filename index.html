Questions from inside Christianity

The familiar answers are where the questions start.

I'm a Christian. This isn't a debunking project, and it isn't conventional apologetics. It's a place to stop at the phrases Christians hear so often that we can forget how strange—and important—they actually are.

“Believe the gospel.” What exactly are we being asked to believe? “Jesus died for your sins.” How does his death affect my sins? “Have faith.” Faith in what—and what is faith supposed to do?

I don't have a tidy answer waiting behind every question. That's sort of the point.
