FAITH

Christianity claims that God exists. That Jesus lived, was crucified, and rose from the dead. That the Bible tells the truth about him. Those are not insignificant claims. They deserve more than blind acceptance—or automatic dismissal. Examine the evidence. Question the assumptions. Follow wherever the facts lead.

HE WAS CRUCIFIED

ROME
CRUCIFIED.

A heel bone from a man crucified in first-century Judea, found at Givat ha-Mivtar in 1968, still held an iron nail. The photograph shows a museum replica. The find is widely cited as evidence of Roman crucifixion practice, though its date and interpretation are debated, and it is not evidence of Jesus' own execution.

HE ROSE FROM THE DEAD

WAS THE
TOMB EMPTY?

The first Christians claimed Jesus rose. This photograph shows the Garden Tomb in Jerusalem, not a confirmed tomb of Jesus; archaeologists such as Gabriel Barkay date it centuries earlier, to the Iron Age, and the Church of the Holy Sepulchre is the older traditional site. Examine the testimony.
