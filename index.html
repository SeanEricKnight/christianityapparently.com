Start with Him

Whatever brought you here,
start with Jesus.

Christianity can get complicated very quickly. Churches. Theology. Politics. Arguments. Denominations. People behaving badly in the name of God.

Before you try to sort all of that out, take another look at Jesus.

Read what He said. Watch who He moved toward. Notice who made Him angry. See what He did with people who had failed badly.
