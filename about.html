What this is

Christianity,
apparently.

A small project about Jesus, faith, doubt, the Bible, and questions worth not pretending we’ve solved.

This exists for people who believe, people who used to believe, people who want to believe, people who are not sure what they believe, and people who are simply curious about Jesus.

The goal is simple.

Point people to Jesus.

Not win every argument. Not turn doubt into a character flaw. Not polish Christianity until it becomes impossible to recognize real life in it.

Why “apparently”?

Because Christians say enormous things so often that we can stop hearing how enormous they are.

God became human. Love your enemies. The dead will rise. Grace is a gift. Jesus is Lord.

It is worth slowing down and asking what any of that actually means.

The videos are short. The questions aren’t.

The YouTube channel is where many of these questions begin. This site is where they get more room.

If something here helps you take one honest step toward Jesus, it did its job.
