Basics

Who is the Holy Spirit?

Not a force, a mood, or the part of church where everyone starts talking more quietly.

Christians believe the Holy Spirit is God, personally present with and in believers.

What does He do?

The New Testament describes the Spirit as teaching, guiding, convicting, comforting, giving gifts, producing character, and pointing people to Jesus.

Christians also believe the Spirit is part of how God changes people from the inside rather than merely handing them rules from the outside.

What should that look like?

Not only dramatic experiences. Paul’s list in Galatians 5 is stubbornly ordinary: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.

If claims about the Spirit produce arrogance, manipulation, or chaos without love, Christians have good reason to be suspicious.
