DON'T CONFUSE
AN ANSWER

Our working rule

Authority may tell you what an institution believes. It cannot, by itself, tell you whether that belief is true. Start with the claim. Identify the evidence. Test the inference.

02 / Christianity under debate

THE CHURCH
COUNCILS.

Did Nicaea choose the Bible? No. The surviving council record says otherwise. Investigate what each council actually decided, what came later, and what popular accounts get wrong.

First, a distinction that matters.
There is no universally agreed list of every “ecumenical” council. Catholic tradition counts 21. Eastern Orthodoxy usually recognizes seven. 06 / How we work

CONVICTION.
Without pretense.

Write with conviction. Investigate with skepticism. Conclude with integrity. A strong argument survives scrutiny. A weak argument deserves correction, regardless of whose side it's on.

01 / STATE THE CLAIM

Be precise.

Define what is being asserted. Don't substitute a slogan for an argument.

02 / EXAMINE THE RECORD

Show your work.

Start with primary texts. Compare independent sources. Name uncertainties.

03 / TEST THE CONCLUSION

Earn the verdict.

Distinguish facts, interpretations, and faith commitments. Revise when better evidence appears.
