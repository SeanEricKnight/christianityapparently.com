First, a distinction that matters.
There is no universally agreed list of every “ecumenical” council. Catholic tradition counts 21. Eastern Orthodoxy usually recognizes seven. The Oriental Orthodox churches and Church of the East follow different histories of reception. This index shows all 21 in the Catholic reckoning, plus significant regional councils below.
