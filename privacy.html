Privacy

Privacy policy.

Short version: this site doesn’t track you, and nothing you do here is sold or shared.

Last updated October 6, 2026. This covers christianityapparently.com and the small YouTube analytics tool I use for my own channel.

This website

Christianity, apparently. is a plain static website. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no ads, no analytics scripts, and it doesn’t set cookies.

Two outside services see basic technical information when you visit, the way they would for any website:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, it handles your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts, so your browser asks Google for them. See Google’s privacy policy ↗.

Links to YouTube, Instagram, X, Discord, GitHub, BibleGateway and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

If you send a suggestion through the “Help make this better” page, it’s posted as a public GitHub issue under your GitHub account, so don’t include anything private.

The YouTube analytics tool

I use a small tool, registered with Google under this site, to read analytics for my own YouTube channel, Christianity Apparently. It is not a public app. The only person who signs in to it is me, with my own Google account.

When I sign in, the tool asks Google for read-only access to my channel’s YouTube data and YouTube Analytics reports, such as video titles, views, watch time and audience retention. It cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on anything.

  • How it’s used: only to show me how my own videos are performing so I can make better ones.
  • Where it’s stored: on my own computer. It isn’t uploaded to this website or any other server I run.
  • Sharing: it isn’t sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and it isn’t used for advertising.
  • AI models: it isn’t used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions ↗, and the stored data is deleted by deleting the tool’s local files.

Christianity, apparently.’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy ↗, including the Limited Use requirements.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using them you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service ↗ and the Google Privacy Policy ↗.

Children

This site doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and questions
