When life hurts

I’m
hurting.

You do not need a theological explanation for every terrible thing before you are allowed to ask God for help.

Some questions are intellectual until the phone rings. Then they are not.

You are allowed to say it hurts.

The Bible does not require cheerful dishonesty. Its prayers contain grief, anger, fear, confusion, accusation, silence, and the question “How long?”

Jesus Himself wept. On the cross He prayed words from Psalm 22: “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”

You do not have to solve suffering tonight.

There are serious Christian answers to the problem of suffering. They matter. But an explanation is not the same thing as comfort, and a person in pain is not a philosophy problem that needs to be won.

Bring God the pain before you can explain the pain.

Ask for help from people, too.

Christianity is not a command to suffer privately. Let people sit with you. Ask for practical help. Talk to someone you trust. If you are in immediate danger, seek immediate help from people who can physically be with you.
