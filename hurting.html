I want to follow Jesus

When life hurts

I’m
hurting.

You can ask God for help without having a theory about why this happened.

Questions about suffering stay theoretical until something actually happens to you.

You don’t have to make this sound spiritual.

The Bible is full of people who are grieving, angry, scared, and confused, and they say so. The Psalms ask God “How long?” over and over. Jesus cried at a friend’s grave.

You can tell God exactly how bad this is.

You don’t have to figure out why this is happening tonight. And please don’t disappear into this by yourself. Tell someone you trust that you’re not doing well.

Tell one person.A friend, your spouse, a pastor, a family member. Pick someone who will actually show up, not just someone who’ll know about it.

Ask for prayer.You don’t need to explain it well. “I’m having a hard time. Will you pray with me?” is enough. If you want someone to pray with you right now, call the Billy Graham 24/7 Prayer Line at 855-255-PRAY (7729).

Get professional help if you need it.Seeing a counselor, therapist, doctor, or support group doesn’t mean your faith failed. Some things are too heavy to handle by yourself, and a trained person may actually help.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988.In the U.S. and its territories, the 988 Lifeline is open 24/7 for emotional distress and crisis support. You don’t have to be suicidal to call. 988 Lifeline ↗
