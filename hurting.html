LIFE IS HARD

I’m hurting.

Christianity does not promise that following Jesus makes life stop hurting.

You are allowed to say this hurts.

The Bible is full of people asking God where He is, why He seems silent, how long this will last, and whether He has forgotten them. Those prayers made it into the Bible.

Life is hard.
Hold on.

You do not need a theological explanation for every terrible thing before you are allowed to ask God for help.

Talk to Him. Tell someone you trust. Let people help you. Eat something. Sleep if you can. Do the next small thing in front of you.

And when you can’t see very far ahead, you don’t have to. Today is enough for today.

Christianity, Apparently.

Point people to Jesus.
