Faith and doubt

Doubt.

Then don’t act certain. Bring the doubt with you and keep looking.

Doubt isn’t one thing. It helps to figure out what you actually doubt.

What do you actually doubt?

God’s existence? The resurrection? The Bible? God’s goodness? Whether He still wants you?

Those are different questions, and they need different answers.

“I believe; help my unbelief.”

A father says that to Jesus in Mark 9. He puts belief and doubt in the same sentence, and Jesus helps him anyway.

Keep looking.

Ask the questions. Read. Think it through. Pray, even if it feels like talking to the ceiling. Don’t shield Christianity from hard questions, and don’t treat how you feel today as the answer.
