Faith and doubt

What if
I doubt?

Then don’t fake certainty. Bring the doubt with you and keep looking.

Doubt is not one thing. Name the actual doubt.

What do you actually doubt?

God’s existence? The resurrection? The Bible? God’s goodness? Whether He still wants you?

Those are different questions. They deserve different answers.

“I believe; help my unbelief.”

That prayer in Mark 9 puts faith and doubt in the same sentence.

Look at it honestly.

Ask questions. Read. Think. Pray honestly. Don’t protect Christianity from hard questions, and don’t mistake a feeling for a verdict.
