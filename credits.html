Where each image comes from, and whether it is a photograph, a replica, or later art.

How to read this

The site’s images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Source records and applicable licence links are included below where checked; Commons file pages remain the primary record. Paintings and manuscript illuminations are later artistic depictions, not photographs of the events they show.

License notes: Where licensed under Creative Commons, links to the license and disclosure of changes are required. The site displays some images cropped or darkened for layout; original files are otherwise not knowingly modified. CC BY-SA 2.0 · CC BY-SA 4.0 · CC BY 2.5 · CC0 · Public-domain mark.
