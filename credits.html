Where each image comes from, and whether it is a photograph, a replica, or later art.

How to read this

The site’s images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Each file page below lists the author and licence, which are the authoritative record. Paintings and manuscript illuminations are later artistic depictions, not photographs of the events they show.
