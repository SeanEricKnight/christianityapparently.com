YOU’RE ALLOWED TO ASK

I have questions.

Good. Questions aren’t the enemy of faith. Pretending you don’t have them might be.

Start with the question you actually have.

GOD

Is God actually there?

Not “what am I supposed to believe?” Start with whether there is anyone there to believe in.

SUFFERING

If God is good, why does this hurt so much?

Christianity doesn’t require you to call pain good. Jesus didn’t.

BIBLE

Can I trust the Bible?

What it is, what it claims, how it got here, and what to do with the difficult parts.

DOUBT

What if I’m not sure anymore?

Then don’t fake certainty. Bring the doubt with you and keep looking.

PRAYER

Why does prayer sometimes feel like talking to the ceiling?

You’re not the first person of faith to wonder whether anyone is listening.

JESUS

Why Jesus?

Before Christianity became a culture, institution, argument, or label, there was a person.
