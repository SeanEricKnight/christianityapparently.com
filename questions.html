You don’t have to stop asking hard questions in order to take Jesus seriously.

Pick the question that is actually bothering you. Not the one you think a Christian website wants you to ask.

Why does a loving, all-powerful God let children suffer?

If God wants us to know Him, why is He so hard to find?

Why doesn’t God answer prayers for things He supposedly wants too?

What happens to someone who honestly searches for God and still can’t believe?

If Christianity is true, why does your birthplace predict your religion so strongly?

Is eternal punishment really just for what someone does in one finite lifetime?
