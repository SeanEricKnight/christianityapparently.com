I want to follow Jesus

I want to follow Jesus

You can start now.

You do not need to clean yourself up first. That would rather defeat the point.

Christianity says salvation is a gift. You receive it by trusting Jesus — who He is, what He did on the cross, and that God raised Him from the dead.

What does that mean?

Repent. Turn toward God. That includes being honest about your sin instead of defending it.

Trust Jesus. Not your religious performance. Not whether you feel sufficiently sincere today. Him.

Ask for forgiveness. Christianity says Jesus died for sinners, which is convenient because that appears to be the available population.

You can tell Him.

There is no required script. If you need words, something this simple is enough:

If you mean it

Jesus, I know I need You. Forgive me. I trust You. Help me follow You.

The words are not magic. You are not entering a password correctly. The prayer is simply saying to God what you are deciding.
