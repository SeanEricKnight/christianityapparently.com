I want to follow Jesus

Life issues

I cannot get past what I did.

Guilt says something was wrong. Shame says you are nothing but the wrong thing.

Christianity takes sin seriously without making your worst moment your permanent name.

Do not rename guilt as nothing.

If you hurt someone, confess it. Make amends where you can. Accept consequences where they belong. Grace is not pretending harm did not happen.

But shame does not get the final word.

The Gospel says forgiveness is received, not achieved by punishing yourself long enough. If Christ forgives sinners, then endless self-condemnation is not humility.

You may need another person in this.

A pastor, counselor, trusted friend, recovery group, or the person you need to apologize to. Some things heal badly in secrecy.
