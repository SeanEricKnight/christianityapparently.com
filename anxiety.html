Life issues

When your mind will not stop.

Prayer can help. So can sleep, a doctor, a therapist, medication, a walk, and another human being.

Anxiety is not proof that your faith is defective.

Start smaller than the whole future.

Ask what you need for the next hour, not whether you can solve the next ten years before dinner.

Tell God what you are afraid of.

Not the sanitized version. Name the thing. The Psalms are full of fear stated plainly.

Use ordinary help too.

Talk to someone you trust. If anxiety is persistent, severe, or interfering with daily life, consider professional care. Christian faith and competent mental-health care are not enemies.

For right now

Jesus, I am afraid. Help me do the next thing in front of me.

If anxiety becomes a crisis, use crisis help.

In the U.S., if you feel unsafe or think you may hurt yourself, call or text 988 for the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services.
