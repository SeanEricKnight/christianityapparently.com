Life issues

Grief is not a problem to solve.

Someone or something mattered. That is why this hurts.

Christian hope does not require pretending loss is smaller than it is.

You are allowed to grieve.

The Bible does not treat grief as weak faith. Jesus wept. The Psalms lament. Paul describes believers as grieving with hope—not as people who do not grieve.

Do not rush yourself into a lesson.

You may eventually see meaning, gratitude, anger, relief, guilt, or all of them in the same hour. You do not need to force a spiritual conclusion tonight.

Stay near people.

Let someone know when the day is bad. Accept meals. Say the person’s name. Tell stories. Ask for prayer if you want it. Grief can make isolation feel easier than company, but disappearing into it rarely helps.

If words are hard

Jesus, this hurts. Please stay close to me in it.
