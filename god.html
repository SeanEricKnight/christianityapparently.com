Who is God?

Christianity starts here, so it is worth being clear about what Christians mean.

Christians believe there is one God: the creator of everything, not part of the universe and not dependent on it.

God is not just a bigger version of us.

The Bible describes God as eternal, holy, just, merciful, personal, and loving. He is not one being among many. He is the reason there is anything at all.

That is a very large claim. Christianity then makes it stranger: this God wants to be known.

Christians think Jesus shows us what God is like.

The New Testament says Jesus is the clearest revelation of God’s character. So Christianity does not ask you to imagine an invisible deity and then assign Him traits. It keeps pointing back to Jesus.

And Christians say God is Trinity.

One God, three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. That needs its own page because pretending the sentence is self-explanatory would be ambitious.
