Life issues

Loneliness can make the whole world feel farther away.

You can be surrounded by people and still feel completely unseen.

Christianity says you were made for relationship with God and with people. That does not make loneliness disappear on command.

Do one social thing before you feel ready.

Text someone. Sit near people. Go to church. Ask one person to get coffee. Isolation tends to argue that reaching out will be awkward. It may be. Awkward is survivable.

Do not confuse being alone with being unwanted.

Loneliness speaks with enormous confidence about what everyone else thinks. It is not always a reliable witness.

Let church be more than a room you enter.

A healthy church should eventually become people who know your name, notice when you are gone, and let you know theirs.
