I want to follow Jesus

Start with Him

Jesus?

Before you decide what you think about Christianity, look at Jesus.

Everything in Christianity depends on one person, so it makes sense to start with Him.

Start with Jesus.

Christians believe Jesus is the Messiah and Son of God, that He died for our sins, and that God raised Him from the dead.

You might not believe that. You don’t have to believe it to take a look.

Read what He said and what He did, and pay attention to who He spent time with.

Read one Gospel.

Mark is short and moves fast. John is slower and more reflective. Either one is a better place to start than random verses, internet arguments, or Christians behaving badly.

Jesus doesn’t ask to be admired. He asks people to trust Him and follow Him.

If you want to begin

Jesus, I want to know who You are. Show me.
