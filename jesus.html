START WITH HIM

I want to know Jesus.

Before you decide what you think about Christianity, take a serious look at Jesus.

Read Him for yourself.

Start with one of the four Gospel accounts: Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John. Mark moves quickly. John spends more time on who Jesus says He is. Pick one and read it like you’re meeting Him, not completing an assignment.

Notice what Jesus says. Who He moves toward. What makes Him angry. Who He forgives. What He asks people to do.

You don’t know Jesus?
Get to know Him.

If you want to talk to Him and don’t know what to say, it doesn’t have to be complicated:

Jesus, have mercy on me.
I believe in You.
Help me follow You.

Those words don’t save you. Jesus does.

You can’t earn your way to Him. Just come.
