Start with Him

Jesus?

Before Christianity was a religion you had an opinion about, there was Jesus.

If you’re going to decide what you think about Christianity, start with the person at the center of it.

Start with Jesus.

Christians believe Jesus is the Messiah and Son of God, that He died for our sins, and that God raised Him from the dead.

You may not believe that. You don’t have to pretend you do in order to look.

Read what He said. Watch what He did. Notice who He moved toward.

Read one Gospel.

Mark is short and direct. John is slower and more reflective. Either is a better place to begin than fragments, arguments, or Christians behaving badly.

Jesus does not merely ask to be admired. He calls people to trust Him and follow Him.

If you want to begin

Jesus, I want to know who You are. Show me.
