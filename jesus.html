I want to follow Jesus

Start with Him

Jesus?

Before you decide what you think about Christianity, look at Jesus.

Before you decide what you think about Christianity, look at Jesus. Everything else hangs on Him.

Christians do not mean “great moral teacher.”

They believe Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, and God the Son: fully human and fully divine. That is a much bigger claim than “He had good ideas about kindness.”

He announced the kingdom of God.

Jesus taught about forgiveness, enemies, money, hypocrisy, prayer, justice, sex, power, mercy, and what it means to love God and your neighbor. He also acted with unusual authority—healing, forgiving sins, challenging religious leaders, and calling people to follow Him personally.

He was crucified.

Jesus was executed by Roman crucifixion under Pontius Pilate. Christians believe His death was not merely a martyrdom but part of salvation: He gave Himself for sinners.

Then comes the claim everything depends on.

His followers said He rose from the dead. Paul is unusually plain about the stakes: if Jesus was not raised, Christian faith collapses.

You do not have to accept that because a Christian website says so. It is exactly the sort of claim you should examine.

Where should I start reading?

Start with Mark. It is fast, concrete, and does not spend much time warming up. Then read John, which spends more time on who Jesus claims to be.
