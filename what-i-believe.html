I want to follow Jesus

What I believe

The ground I’m standing on.

This site asks hard questions, but it is not pretending to be religiously neutral.

I’m writing from inside Christianity. These are the basic convictions the rest of the site assumes.

God

There is one God, creator of everything, who is holy, just, merciful, loving, and personal. Christians speak of this one God as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus

Jesus is not merely a teacher I admire. Christianity says He is the Son of God, fully human and fully divine. He lived, was crucified, died for people like us, and rose from the dead.

Being made right with God

We do not earn our way back to God. Forgiveness is a gift, received by turning to Jesus and trusting Him.

The Bible

I treat the Bible as authoritative for Christian faith while also taking seriously that it was written through real human authors, languages, cultures, genres, and history. Taking it seriously includes doing the work of interpretation instead of pretending every difficult passage is easy.

The church

Christians are meant to follow Jesus with other Christians, not as isolated individuals. Churches can also fail badly, which is why “go to church” is not the same thing as “trust every church.”

The future

Christian hope is resurrection, judgment, and God finally putting creation right—not merely escaping the world after death.

Where Christians disagree

Baptism, communion, church government, predestination, spiritual gifts, the age of the earth, details of the afterlife, and plenty more. I’ll try to name those disagreements instead of quietly presenting one tradition’s answer as though nobody else exists.

If a page on this site goes beyond these basics, it should tell you when Christians disagree and when I’m less certain.
