Grace

What does it mean
to be saved?

You don’t earn salvation. You receive it by trusting Jesus.

Saved from what? By whom? How?

Grace starts where earning ends.

Christianity says sin has broken our relationship with God, and good behavior cannot repair it.

God acted first. Jesus died for our sins and rose from the dead.

Salvation is not wages. It is gift.

So what do I do?

Trust Jesus. Turn toward Him. Receive mercy. Begin to follow Him.

If you believe

Jesus, I believe. Save me.

The words don’t save you. Jesus does.

Then keep following: prayer, Scripture, baptism, church, love, confession, grace.
