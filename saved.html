Grace

What does it mean
to be saved?

You don’t earn salvation. You receive it by trusting Jesus.

“Saved” is one of those church words people use without explaining. Saved from what, by whom, and how?

You can’t earn it.

Christianity says sin has broken our relationship with God, and being good can’t fix that.

God acted first. Jesus died for our sins and rose from the dead.

Salvation isn’t a paycheck for good behavior. It’s a gift.

So what do I do?

Trust Jesus. Repentance means turning toward God instead of defending your sin or trying to save yourself.

This page explains the word saved. If you are asking how to become a Christian, the Decision page is the place to go. It keeps the response in one place instead of giving you three slightly different versions of the same prayer.
