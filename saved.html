What does it mean
to be saved?

You do not earn your way back to God. You receive forgiveness by trusting Jesus.

“Saved” is one of those church words people use without explaining. So what is someone being saved from, who is doing the saving, and what does it change?

You can’t earn it.

Christianity says we have broken our relationship with God, and being good cannot repair it.

God acted first. Jesus died for what we have done and rose from the dead.

Being made right with God is not a paycheck for good behavior. It is a gift.

So what do I do?

Trust Jesus. Turn toward God instead of defending what you know is wrong or trying to rescue yourself. Christians often call that change of direction repentance.
