Grace

What does it mean
to be saved?

You don’t earn salvation. You receive it by trusting Jesus.

“Saved” is one of those church words people use without explaining. Saved from what, by whom, and how?

You can’t earn it.

Christianity says sin has broken our relationship with God, and being good can’t fix that.

God acted first. Jesus died for our sins and rose from the dead.

Salvation isn’t a paycheck for good behavior. It’s a gift.

So what do I do?

Trust Jesus. Turn toward Him, and accept the forgiveness He offers. Then start following Him.

Repentance means turning away from sin and toward God. You don’t clean yourself up first. You can’t, which is why you turn to Him.

If you believe

Jesus, I believe. Save me.

The words aren’t magic. Jesus is the one who saves.

After that you keep following Him: praying, reading Scripture, getting baptized, joining a church, and loving people. When you fail, admit it and keep going.
