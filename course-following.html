I want to follow Jesus

Course 03 · 5 lessons

Follow Jesus on ordinary Tuesdays.

Most Christian growth is not dramatic. It is ordinary faithfulness repeated long enough to become a life.

About 45–60 minutesFor new believers and people starting again
01

Lesson 1

Talk to God honestly.

Prayer is not a performance review. Thank God, ask for help, admit what is wrong, complain when you need to, and borrow words from the Psalms when yours disappear.

Matthew 6:9–13 · Romans 8:26 (NLT) ↗

Do this: spend ten minutes praying without trying to sound religious.

How do I pray? →What about unanswered prayer? →
02

Lesson 2

Do not try to do Christianity alone.

Find Christians you can actually know. A healthy church should take Jesus and the Bible seriously, treat people with mercy, have accountable leadership, and survive honest questions.

Acts 2:42–47 · Hebrews 10:24–25 · 1 Peter 5:1–3 (NLT) ↗

Do this: use the church page to identify one congregation worth visiting.

Find a church →
03

Lesson 3

Learn the practices Christians inherited.

Baptism and communion are old, physical practices Jesus gave His followers. Christians disagree about important details, but almost every historic tradition treats them as significant.

Matthew 28:19 · Romans 6:3–4 · 1 Corinthians 11:23–26 (NLT) ↗

Do this: read both pages, then ask a church how it understands and practices them.

What is baptism? →What is communion? →
04

Lesson 4

Pay attention to the person you are becoming.

Christian growth is not merely knowing more. It should gradually show up as love, patience, honesty, generosity, mercy, self-control, repentance, and a willingness to make things right.

Galatians 5:22–23 · Matthew 22:37–40 · James 1:22 (NLT) ↗

Do this: pick one relationship where following Jesus should change your behavior this week.

Who is the Holy Spirit? →Keep growing →
05

Lesson 5

Expect doubt, failure, and seasons when you drift.

You will not execute Christianity flawlessly. Doubt is not automatically apostasy, failure is not the end of the story, and returning to God does not require pretending you never wandered.

Mark 9:24 · 1 John 1:9 · John 21:15–19 (NLT) ↗

Do this: choose the page that sounds most like where you actually are and start there.

I have doubts →I walked away →I cannot get past what I did →

Finished?

Keep doing the boring things.

Prayer, Scripture, church, repentance, love, generosity, perseverance. Spectacularly unmarketable. Still the work.
