Following Jesus

Okay.
What now?

Do the ordinary things. Keep doing them. Grace is not only how you start.

You do not need to become a different species of person by Thursday. Following Jesus is a life, not a launch event.

Talk to God.

Pray. Not impressively. Honestly. Thank Him. Ask for help. Confess sin. Sit in silence. Learn the Lord’s Prayer. Borrow words from the Psalms when yours run out.

Read the Gospels.

Stay close to Jesus. Read Mark, Luke, John, Matthew. Read slowly enough to notice what surprises you instead of racing to finish a chapter.

Find other Christians.

Christianity was never intended to be practiced entirely alone.

Find a good church. Not a perfect one. You’ll be looking until the heat death of the universe.

Look for a church that takes Jesus and Scripture seriously, practices grace rather than merely talking about it, and is safe enough for honest questions.

Be baptized.

Jesus told His followers to be baptized. Christian traditions differ on some details of baptism, but if you have decided to follow Him and have not been baptized, talk to a church about it.

Learn to love.

Love God. Love your neighbor. Forgive. Tell the truth. Be generous. Care about the person everybody else walks past. None of this earns salvation. It is what life with Jesus begins to look like.

When you fail, get up.

You will fail. Confess it. Receive grace. Make things right where you can. Keep following.

Christian maturity is not never falling. It is learning where to go when you do.
