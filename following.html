KEEP GOING

I’m trying to follow Him.

Following Jesus is not the same thing as never failing again.

Do the ordinary things.

01

Talk to God.

Not perform. Talk. Tell Him the truth.

02

Read about Jesus.

Start with the Gospels. Keep coming back to what He actually said and did.

03

Find a good church.

Not a perfect one. You’ll be looking until the heat death of the universe.

04

Find Christians you trust.

Faith was never designed as a solitary hobby.

05

Get baptized.

If you’ve chosen to follow Jesus and haven’t been baptized, talk to a church about it.

06

Love people.

Especially when it is inconvenient.

07

Forgive people.

That does not mean pretending what happened was okay.

08

When you fall, get up.

Grace is not permission to stop caring. It is why failure does not have to be the end.

This gets complicated. The center doesn’t have to.

Follow Jesus.
