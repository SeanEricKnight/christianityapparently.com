Following Jesus

Okay.
What now?

Mostly it’s ordinary things, done over a long time.

You don’t need to become a different species of person by Thursday. Following Jesus is mostly ordinary things done for a long time.

Talk to God.

Pray. You don’t have to be impressive about it. Be honest. Thank Him, ask for help, admit what you’ve done wrong, or just sit there quietly. Learn the Lord’s Prayer. When you run out of words, borrow some from the Psalms.

Read the Gospels.

Read Mark, Luke, John, and Matthew. Read slowly enough to notice what surprises you instead of racing to finish a chapter.

Find other Christians.

Christianity wasn’t meant to be done alone.

Find a good church. Not a perfect one. You’ll be looking until the heat death of the universe.

Look for a church that takes Jesus and Scripture seriously, treats people who fail with mercy instead of just talking about mercy, and lets people ask honest questions without treating them like a problem.

Be baptized.

Jesus told His followers to be baptized. Christian traditions differ on some details of baptism, but if you have decided to follow Him and have not been baptized, talk to a church about it.

Learn to love.

Love God and love your neighbor. Forgive people. Tell the truth. Be generous. Pay attention to the person everyone else walks past. None of this earns you salvation. It’s what life with Jesus starts to look like.

When you fail, get up.

You’ll fail at this. Admit it, accept the forgiveness Jesus offers, and make things right where you can. Then keep following Him.
