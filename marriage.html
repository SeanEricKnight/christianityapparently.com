Life issues

Marriage trouble is not one thing.

Disconnection, betrayal, contempt, addiction, fear, and abuse are not interchangeable problems.

“Try harder” is not a marriage plan.

Name what is happening.

Are you drifting? Fighting constantly? Hiding something? Recovering from infidelity? Afraid of your spouse? The next step depends on the actual problem.

Get help before the crisis chooses the timing.

A skilled counselor, pastor, or mature couple can help you see patterns that are hard to see from inside them.

Safety changes the advice.

If there is violence, coercion, threats, stalking, or fear for your safety, this is not merely a communication problem. Prioritize safety and involve qualified local help.

Christian teaching about covenant should never be used to pressure someone to remain in immediate danger.
