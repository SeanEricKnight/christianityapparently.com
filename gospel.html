I want to follow Jesus

The Gospel

The center of Christianity, step by step.

Six steps. No assumption that you already know the church words.

01

God

God made you, and He is not far away.

Christianity starts with God. It says He made us, knows us, and made us to know Him.

That means your life is not an accident and God is not just an emergency number for when things go sideways.

Genesis 1:26–27 · Acts 17:27 ↗

Stuck here? Why is God so hard to find?

02

Something is broken

The world is a mess. We are not innocent bystanders.

Christianity calls the problem sin. The word has baggage, but the claim is straightforward: we do not love God or other people as we should. We lie, use people, protect ourselves, worship smaller things, and help make a broken world more broken.

Sin is not merely “breaking religious rules.” Christianity says it separates us from God, and we cannot repair that separation by becoming slightly nicer.

Romans 3:23 · Romans 6:23 ↗

Stuck here? What does Christianity mean by good and evil?

03

Jesus

Christianity says God came after us.

Jesus did not arrive to give decent people a final improvement plan. Christians believe He is God the Son, that He lived without sin and willingly died for sinners.

The cross is where Christianity says justice and mercy meet. Christians do not believe God ignored evil. They believe He dealt with it at enormous cost.

Romans 5:8 · Isaiah 53:5–6 ↗

Stuck here? Why did Jesus have to die?

04

Resurrection

Then Christians make the strange claim: He got up.

The resurrection is not a decorative ending to the story. Paul says if Christ was not raised, Christian faith collapses.

Christians believe Jesus was buried and physically raised from the dead. That is why they call Him Lord rather than merely a teacher worth quoting.

1 Corinthians 15:3–8, 14 ↗

Stuck here? What evidence is there for the resurrection?

05

Your response

You actually have to do something with this.

The Christian invitation is to repent — turn around — and trust Jesus. Faith is not pretending to feel certain. It is putting your trust in Him rather than in your own record.

Salvation is received, not earned. Christians believe forgiveness is a gift because Jesus did what we could not do for ourselves.

Mark 1:15 · Ephesians 2:8–9 ↗

Not ready? What if I honestly cannot believe?

06

Tell Him

If you mean it, say it to God.

You do not need a perfect prayer. You can tell God the truth: that you need Him, that you trust Jesus, that you want forgiveness, and that you want to follow Him.

If you need words

Jesus, I know I need You. Forgive me. I trust You. Help me follow You.

The words are not magic. The prayer is not a password. It is simply saying to God what you are deciding.

Romans 10:9–13 ↗
