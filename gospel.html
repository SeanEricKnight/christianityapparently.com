The Gospel

Christianity in four moves.

Not everything Christians believe. Just the center of it.

01

God

You were made for more than surviving the week.

Christianity begins with God, not with a list of things you did wrong. It says God made us, knows us, and made us to know Him.

That means your life is not an accident and God is not merely an emergency service for when things go sideways.

Genesis 1:26–27 · John 17:3 ↗

02

Us

Something is wrong. We’re part of it.

Christianity calls it sin. The word has baggage, but the basic claim is not complicated: we do not love God or other people as we should. We lie, use people, protect ourselves, worship smaller things, and help make a broken world more broken.

Sin is not merely “breaking religious rules.” It separates us from God and leads somewhere we cannot repair by becoming slightly nicer.

Romans 3:23 · Romans 6:23 ↗

03

Jesus

Christianity says God came after us.

Jesus did not arrive to give decent people a final improvement plan. Christians believe He is God the Son, that He lived the life we have not, died for our sins, and rose from the dead.

The cross is where Christianity says judgment and mercy meet. The resurrection is why Christians think Jesus is not merely a dead teacher with unusually persistent followers.

Romans 5:8 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 ↗

04

You

You can actually respond to this.

The Christian invitation is to repent — turn around — and trust Jesus. You do not earn your way in. You receive forgiveness and begin following Him.

You can talk to Him plainly: admit what is true, ask for forgiveness, and put your trust in Him. The words are not magic. Jesus is the one Christians believe saves.

Romans 10:9–13 · Ephesians 2:8–9 ↗

So now what?

You do not have to fake certainty.
