Christian practice

What is communion?

Bread, wine, Jesus’ last meal—and one of Christianity’s oldest practices.

Communion, the Lord’s Supper, and the Eucharist are names Christians use for the meal Jesus gave His disciples before His death.

What is it for?

Christians remember Jesus’ death, proclaim what He did, give thanks, and share the meal as a church.

What happens in it?

Christians disagree. Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Baptist, Anglican, and other traditions explain Christ’s presence in the meal differently.

The disagreement is not proof that the practice is unimportant. If anything, two thousand years of arguing about bread is evidence that Christians have thought it matters quite a lot.

Should I take communion anywhere?

Ask the church you are visiting. Different churches have different expectations about baptism, membership, and who should participate.
