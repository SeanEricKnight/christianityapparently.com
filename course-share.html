I want to follow Jesus

Course 04 · 4 lessons

Talk about Jesus without turning people into projects.

You do not need a sales funnel for the Messiah. Listen well, say what you believe clearly, and leave room for the other person to be a person.

About 30–45 minutesFor ordinary conversations, not stage microphones
01

Lesson 1

Listen before you explain.

People are not objections with shoes on. Ask what they actually believe, what they have experienced, and what bothers them about Christianity before answering the question you hoped they would ask.

James 1:19 · 1 Peter 3:15–16 (NLT) ↗

Do this: have one conversation where your goal is understanding rather than winning.

Read: Point people to Jesus →
02

Lesson 2

Know how to explain the Gospel in normal language.

You should be able to explain the center without hiding behind church vocabulary: God made us for Himself; we are part of what has gone wrong; Jesus came after us, died, rose, and calls us to trust Him.

1 Corinthians 15:3–4 · Romans 5:8 · Romans 10:9–13 (NLT) ↗

Do this: walk through the Gospel and practice saying each step in your own words.

Review the Gospel →What does “saved” mean? →
03

Lesson 3

Tell your story without making yourself the evidence.

Your experience matters because it is yours. It is not proof that Christianity is true. Say what changed, what did not magically change, why you trust Jesus, and where you still have questions.

1 Peter 3:15 · John 9:24–25 (NLT) ↗

Do this: write three sentences: what you thought before, what changed your mind or drew you toward Jesus, and what following Him looks like now.

See the site's posture →Be ready for real questions →
04

Lesson 4

Stick around after the conversation.

If someone wants to keep exploring, help them read a Gospel, answer what you can, admit what you cannot, and help them meet healthy Christians. If they are not interested, remain their friend. This should not be complicated.

Matthew 28:19–20 · 1 Thessalonians 2:8 (NLT) ↗

Do this: know the next page you would send someone based on where they are: Jesus, Gospel, Questions, Life Issues, or Church.

Jesus →Questions →Life Issues →Find a church →

Finished?

Good. Now be normal.

Love people. Tell the truth. Answer when asked. Admit when you do not know. You are not the Holy Spirit with a clipboard.
