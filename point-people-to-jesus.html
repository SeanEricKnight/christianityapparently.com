Make it apparent

Point people
to Jesus.

With your words, yes. But also with the kind of life that makes people wonder where the love came from.

If Jesus is who Christians say He is, faith should eventually become visible.

Live something worth asking about.

Jesus said His followers’ light should be seen so attention moves toward God (Matthew 5:16), and that people would recognize His disciples by their love (John 13:35).

Patience, mercy, honesty, generosity, repentance, and love should make the explanation believable.

If your faith is real, it should show.

Not as performance. Forgive when revenge would be easier. Tell the truth when lying would help. Admit when you are wrong. Notice the person everyone else ignores.

Not to earn salvation. Grace leaves fingerprints.

And sometimes, say His name.

Tell the truth plainly: who Jesus is, what He has done, why you trust Him, and what grace has meant in your life.

You are not required to win the argument. You are not the Holy Spirit with a clipboard.

The Romans Road.

A simple path through the gospel using Paul’s letter to the Romans.

THE PROBLEM

Romans 3:23

All of us have sinned. Nobody arrives because they were the impressive one.

THE CONSEQUENCE

Romans 6:23

Sin leads to death, but God offers eternal life as a gift in Jesus Christ.

THE LOVE

Romans 5:8

Christ died for us while we were still sinners.

THE RESPONSE

Romans 10:9–10

Confess Jesus as Lord and believe that God raised Him from the dead.

THE INVITATION

Romans 10:13

Everyone who calls on the Lord can be saved.

It is a road, not a spell.

Those verses are not magic words. Jesus saves.

If someone has questions, let them ask. If they are hurting, listen before turning their pain into an opening statement.

Then keep living it.

Walk with people. Pray with them. Help them read Scripture. Help them find a healthy church. Keep showing what grace looks like when people fail and get back up.

Look at Jesus. Ask what’s true. Trust Him. Then make it apparent.
