I want to follow Jesus

Make it apparent

Point people
to Jesus.

With your words, yes. But also with a life that makes people curious why you act the way you do.

If Jesus is who Christians say He is, it should eventually show up in how you live.

Live in a way people might ask about.

Jesus said people should be able to see the good His followers do and end up giving God the credit (Matthew 5:16), and that people would recognize His disciples by their love (John 13:35).

When you’re patient, merciful, honest, and generous, and when you apologize if you’re wrong, what you say about Jesus is easier to believe.

This isn’t for show. It looks like forgiving someone when revenge would be easier, telling the truth when a lie would be more convenient, admitting when you’re wrong, and noticing the person everyone else ignores. None of that earns you salvation. It’s just what you’d expect from someone who’s been forgiven a lot: less grudge-holding, less defensiveness about being wrong, a little more generosity.

And sometimes you’ll need to say it out loud.

Say what you actually believe: who Jesus is, what He did, why you trust Him, and what’s changed for you because of it.

You don’t have to win the argument. You are not the Holy Spirit with a clipboard.

The Romans Road.

A short walk through the gospel using five passages from Paul’s letter to the Romans.

THE PROBLEM

Romans 3:23

Everyone has sinned and falls short of God’s glory, so nobody gets there by being the impressive one.

THE CONSEQUENCE

Romans 6:23

Sin leads to death, but God offers eternal life as a gift in Jesus Christ.

THE LOVE

Romans 5:8

Christ died for us while we were still sinners.

THE RESPONSE

Romans 10:9–10

Confess Jesus as Lord and believe that God raised Him from the dead.

THE INVITATION

Romans 10:13

Everyone who calls on the Lord can be saved.

It’s a road, not a spell.

Those verses aren’t magic words. Jesus is the one who saves.

If someone has questions, let them ask. If they’re hurting, listen before you turn their pain into an opening statement.

Then keep living it.

Stick around. Pray with them, help them read Scripture, help them find a healthy church. And when they fail, which they will, treat them the way you’d want to be treated.

That’s the whole idea behind this site: look at Jesus, ask whether it’s true, trust Him if it is, and let it show in how you live.
