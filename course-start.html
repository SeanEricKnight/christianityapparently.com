I want to follow Jesus

Course 01 · 4 lessons

Start with Jesus.

You do not need to understand every Christian doctrine before looking at the person everything depends on.

About 30–45 minutesFor curious people and new believers
01

Lesson 1

Look at Jesus before you decide what you think about Christianity.

Christianity is not mainly a philosophy with Jesus added as the mascot. It makes claims about who He is: Messiah, Son of God, God the Son, crucified and raised.

John 1:1–14 · Mark 8:27–31 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–8 (NLT) ↗

Do this: read the Jesus page, then read Mark 1 slowly. Notice what Jesus says, what He does, and how people react to Him.

Read: Jesus? →Read Mark 1 ↗
02

Lesson 2

Understand the claim Christians call the Gospel.

The Gospel is the center: God made us for Himself, something has gone wrong between us and Him, Jesus came after us, died, rose, and calls us to trust Him.

Romans 5:8 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 · Ephesians 2:8–9 (NLT) ↗

Do this: walk through the six-step Gospel page. Stop where you actually get stuck instead of hurrying to the end.

Walk through the Gospel →Open the hard questions →
03

Lesson 3

Know what Christians mean by trust.

Trust is not forcing yourself to feel certain. It is relying on Jesus instead of your own record, turning toward God, and receiving forgiveness as a gift rather than an achievement.

Mark 1:15 · Romans 10:9–13 · Ephesians 2:8–9 (NLT) ↗

Do this: read what the site means by being “saved.” If you are ready to make that decision, use the Decision page rather than trying to manufacture a dramatic moment.

What does “saved” mean? →I want to follow Jesus →
04

Lesson 4

Take the first ordinary steps.

Christian faith quickly becomes ordinary practice: talk to God, read the Gospels, find other Christians, be baptized, learn to love people, and get back up when you fail.

Acts 2:42 · Matthew 28:19–20 · John 13:34–35 (NLT) ↗

Do this: read the first-step guide and choose one thing you will actually do this week.

I decided. What now? →Continue to Course 03 →

Finished?

You do not need a certificate.

If the claims about Jesus are still the thing bothering you, keep investigating. If you trust Him, the next course is about actually living that out.
