I want to follow Jesus

Coming back

I walked away.
Now what?

You don’t have to pretend you never left.

Maybe you stopped believing. Maybe church hurt you. Maybe you just drifted.

Start by saying what happened.

You don’t need a respectable reason for why you left. Just say what actually happened.

If church people hurt you, you can come back to Jesus without pretending they didn’t.

Coming back to Jesus doesn’t mean going back to that church, or that group, or those people.

Come back badly.

Pray, even if it’s been years. Read one Gospel. Tell one Christian you trust, “I think I want to come back.” It’ll be awkward, and that’s fine.

You can start here

Jesus, I left. Help me come back.
