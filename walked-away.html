Coming back

I walked away.
Now what?

You don’t have to pretend you never left.

Maybe you stopped believing. Maybe church hurt you. Maybe you simply drifted.

Start with the truth.

You do not need a respectable explanation for why you left. Say what is true.

If church people hurt you, coming back to Jesus does not require pretending they didn’t.

Coming back is not the same thing as pretending nothing happened.

Come back badly.

Pray after years of not praying. Read one Gospel. Tell one trustworthy Christian, “I think I want to come back.”

You can start here

Jesus, I left. Help me come back.
