YOU CAN COME BACK

I walked away.

Maybe you left a church. Maybe you left Christianity. Maybe you just stopped talking to God one day and never really started again.

You don’t have to pretend you never left.

Whatever happened, start with the truth. Anger. disappointment. boredom. hurt. doubt. shame. None of it is improved by dressing it up before you bring it to God.

You left.
Come back.

Coming back doesn’t mean having everything fixed. It can be as small as talking to God again. Reading one of the Gospels. Walking into a church. Telling a Christian you trust what actually happened.

And if church people hurt you, coming back to Jesus does not require pretending they didn’t.
