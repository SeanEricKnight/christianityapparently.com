Coming back

I walked away.
Now what?

You don’t have to pretend you never left.

Maybe you stopped believing. Maybe you were angry. Maybe church hurt you. Maybe nothing dramatic happened at all. You just drifted until Christianity became something that used to matter.

Start with the truth.

You do not need a respectable explanation for why you left. Anger. Disappointment. Boredom. Sin. Confusion. Bad teaching. A thousand small choices. Say what is true.

If church people hurt you, coming back to Jesus does not require pretending they didn’t.

Coming back is not the same thing as pretending nothing happened.

You are not required to clean up first.

The Christian story is not about people getting themselves presentable enough for God to let them back inside.

Jesus’ story of the prodigal son is almost embarrassingly direct: the son comes home rehearsing a speech. The father runs to meet him before he can finish it.

So come back badly.

Pray after years of not praying. Read one Gospel even if you feel nothing. Walk into a church and sit in the back if that is all you can manage. Tell one trustworthy Christian, “I think I want to come back.”

You can start here

Jesus, I left. I don’t know exactly how to come back. Help me.

You do not have to manufacture a dramatic spiritual moment. Take the next honest step.
