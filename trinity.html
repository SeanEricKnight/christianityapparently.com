Basics

What is the Trinity?

One God. Three persons. Not three gods, and not one person wearing three hats.

The Trinity is Christianity trying to hold together what the Bible says about the Father, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit without flattening any of it.

One God.

Christianity remains monotheistic. The Father is God, the Son is God, the Holy Spirit is God, and yet Christians do not mean three separate gods.

Three persons.

The Father is not the Son. The Son is not the Spirit. They relate to one another, speak to one another, and are not interchangeable characters.

No analogy really fixes it.

Water, eggs, clover leaves, the sun—most popular analogies eventually distort something. The doctrine is easier to state than to picture.

That is not the same as saying it is meaningless. It means Christians believe God’s inner life is not simple enough to fit neatly into an object lesson.
